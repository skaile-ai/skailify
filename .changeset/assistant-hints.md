An `assistant` option tells Skaile where to put its agent button over the app (`{ kind: "floating", corner, offset, size, accent }`), or to leave it out (`{ kind: "none" }`). `setAssistant(...)` changes it while the app runs. The peer `@skaile/workspaces` range now starts at 10.14.0, the first version that sends these hints.
